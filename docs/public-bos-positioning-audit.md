# Public BOS Positioning Audit

Date: 2026-07-13

## Goal

Move public messaging from POS-only positioning to AndgatePOS as an SME Business Operating System, while keeping POS keywords where they are intentionally used for search traffic.

## Updated Brand-Level Surfaces

- Homepage metadata, OpenGraph copy, schema and hidden H1 now position AndgatePOS as an SME Business Operating System.
- Global site metadata, Twitter/OpenGraph copy and organization schema now describe Business OS, POS, inventory, ecommerce and operations software.
- Homepage visible copy now says "SME Business OS Bangladesh" and "From POS checkout to complete business operations."
- English and Bangla homepage locale copy now explains POS as one module inside the wider business system.
- Main FAQ now asks whether AndgatePOS is only POS software and answers that it also covers inventory, purchase, CRM, supplier dues, cash closing, HR, reports, ecommerce and daily operations.
- Training page copy now uses Business OS Training Center language and includes registration/login in the learning path.
- User guide metadata and downloadable guide intro now position AndgatePOS as Business OS with POS as a core module.
- Contact, Blog, Landing index, Promotion POS, Affiliate and Comparison pages were updated to avoid POS-only brand framing.
- Reports feature metadata now says Business Reports & Accounting instead of POS Reports.
- Shared SEO schema category for general landing/high-intent pages now uses SME Business Operating System.

## Intentionally Kept POS Terms

These should remain because they target buyer search intent or describe the actual POS module:

- `/features/pos`
- `/pos-software-bangladesh`
- `/retail-pos-software-bangladesh`
- `/restaurant-pos-software-bangladesh`
- `/pharmacy-pos-software-bangladesh`
- `/grocery-pos-software-bangladesh`
- `/best-pos-software-bangladesh`
- `/free-pos-software-bangladesh`
- POS-focused blog articles and high-intent SEO pages
- Keyword arrays such as `BD_KEYWORDS`, where POS search volume is intentional

## Recommendation

Keep the current brand as AndgatePOS until the domain shift is ready, but keep using this transition line across public content:

> POS is one module. AndgatePOS is the SME Business Operating System for daily business operations.

When `andgatebos.com` is ready, the next step should be a domain migration checklist covering canonical URLs, redirects, sitemap, OpenGraph URLs, PWA metadata, email templates, backend CORS/app URLs and analytics tracking.
